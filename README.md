## 💡 About The Project

Ever get tired of setting reminders in traditional to-do apps, only to accidentally ignore the notifications and miss your deadlines? **TaskFlow** solves this problem by taking control of the entire reminder flow. 

It acts as your personal AI task assistant: you can talk to it naturally using **Voice Mode** (just like Siri or Alexa) to schedule things, and when it's time, it ensures you notice by reaching out across **multiple communication channels**.

---

## ✨ Key Features

- **🎙️ Voice-Activated Scheduling:** Hands-free task creation using voice control. Speak naturally to add, edit, or manage your schedule on the go.
- **📲 Multi-Channel Alert System:** Never miss a critical task again. When a deadline hits, TaskFlow notifies you via:
  - 📩 **SMS Alerts**
  - 🔔 **Push Notifications**
  - 📞 **Automated Phone Calls**
- **⚡ Smart Task Flow Agent:** Automatically organizes, prioritizes, and triggers alerts based on your real-time routine.
